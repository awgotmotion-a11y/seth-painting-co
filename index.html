How We Work

Our 4-Step Process

1

Free Estimate

We come to you, assess the job in detail, and hand you a clear, itemized written quote — no vague ballparks, no surprise add-ons later.

2

Surface Prep

We pressure wash, sand, scrape, fill cracks, and prime every surface. Paint is only as good as the prep underneath it.

3

Paint Application

Premium paints applied with precision — brushed, rolled, or sprayed depending on the surface. Multiple coats where needed.

4

Final Walkthrough

We walk the job with you before we pack up. If anything isn't right, we fix it on the spot — before we consider the job done.
